# The Ocean's Hidden Response

An English-language scientific investigation tool for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, Yogyakarta. Visitors examine historical surface chlorophyll-a and sea surface temperature in two fixed Pacific regions, compare long-term chlorophyll trends directly, and inspect exploratory date windows. Scientific reviewers can reproduce the calculations.

## Approved product decisions

- Semantic HTML, CSS, modular vanilla JavaScript, and an offline Python reference pipeline. No accounts, database, server backend, forecasting, or 3D globe.
- Chart first, with a supporting geographic locator. Plain explanations precede expandable technical details.
- Copernicus C3S Ocean Colour v6.0 preferred; NOAA Suomi-NPP VIIRS is the approved fallback. NASA JPL MUR monthly SST is the companion candidate. Actual NASA challenge-resource compliance must be demonstrated.
- Fixed, verified reference investigation and visibly exploratory custom date windows. Unsupported significance is unavailable with a reason. Never compare separate p-values as a test of regional difference.
- Authentic data, documented quality and area weighting, no silent imputation, consistent units and source attribution.
- Local work initially. Credentials stay outside the repository. Codex Cloud is deferred.
- Original overall archival visual language inspired by Internet Archive, with charts primary. No copied branding or assets.
- Seven-slide English demo, subject to the final submission portal. Publication and submission need separate authorization.
