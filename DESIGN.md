# Visual direction

Implementation choices under the user's approved overall archival visual language, preserving the chart-first scientific journey. This is an original interface, not a recreation of Internet Archive branding or layouts.

Mode: Operate with supporting Read sections. ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1. The primary focal point is the historical chart. Dense record controls and metadata give way to quieter explanatory reading below. Functional hover/focus transitions only.

## System

- Paper background `#f7f6f1`, white chart, ink `#242b2c`, secondary ink `#4c5556`, rule `#c7cbc7`. The pale surface supports prolonged daytime reading and archival character.
- EEP `#963e30` (rust) and NWP `#1a6266` (deep teal), always paired with labels and distinct line patterns. These identify records, not significance or good/bad outcomes.
- Restrained dark masthead. Links underlined with visible focus. No decorative badges, icons, gradients, glows, shadows, giant hero, or card-grid composition.
- Serif title and reading headings, clear sans-serif controls/body, tabular numerals for observations. Use locally available Georgia for the archival title and a readable system body stack; chart text remains a minimum readable size. No external font requests.
- A central working area combines the variable/region/date controls, reference/exploratory state, chart, and compact trend table. A smaller supporting map and provenance record must not compete with the chart.
- A continuous responsive range: controls wrap at content breakpoints; chart/result table has an accessible narrow-screen treatment; supporting content moves below the working area. No page-level horizontal overflow. Tap targets at least 44px.

## States and honesty

All chart states include a text description. Missing data breaks lines. Unavailable inference explains the reason without retaining old numbers. Window changes recompute the entire investigation; reference reset restores every control and result. Lag-sensitive significance is explicitly qualified. No invented findings, deployment links, or prototype metrics.
